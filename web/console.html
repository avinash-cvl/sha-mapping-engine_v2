A rejection is a verdict on the pairing, not the listing — the SKU stays eligible and a re-run will process it again. What matters is that it does not land on the same product code twice. Reject a match from the Results table on New run.
Clear and re-run

Step 1 deletes the rejected mapping rows and returns their SKUs to PENDING. Step 2 runs the engine over exactly those SKUs. They are separate because the first is not reversible, and because clearing the rejection is what makes the SKUs eligible for the second.
